# Companion papers

The FunGen-xQTL resources on this site are described in a set of companion papers from the ADSP Functional Genomics Consortium. Each entry below names the paper, says whether it is public yet, and links to the datasets on this site and the xQTL protocol modules that use it. Papers marked "manuscript" are not public yet; their numbers are quoted on this site only where the dataset page says so.

## Resource and flagship papers

- **Broad and deep dissection of Alzheimer's disease loci through integrative xQTL modeling.** The FunGen-AD Consortium. Manuscript. The AD GWAS and xQTL integration behind the [AD Loci Integration Summary](https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn69865823), the [Data catalog](xqtl-data) counts on the home page, and the [AD Loci Explorer](https://xqtl.niagads.org/AD-loci-explorer/).
- **A TAD-informed aging-brain xQTL atlas of multi-modal and cell-type-resolved regulatory variation.** Cifello, Feng, Grenn et al. medRxiv (2026), [doi:10.64898/2026.05.21.26353713](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.21.26353713). The FunGen-xQTL Atlas of association results released on NIAGADS ([How to get the data](how_to_get_data)) and the TAD-boundary-enhanced cis windows used in every QTL scan.
- **A multi-context regulome-wide association atlas for genetic studies of aging brain disorders.** Liu, Wang, Sun et al. medRxiv (2026), [doi:10.64898/2026.05.15.26353329](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.15.26353329). The [FGMB atlas](fgmb_weights_database) of TWAS prediction models. Protocol: [TWAS, cTWAS and MR](https://github.com/StatFunGen/xqtl-protocol/blob/main/code/SoS/pecotmr_integration/twas_ctwas.ipynb).

## Molecular phenotypes

- **Genetic and functional analysis of unproductive splicing using LeafCutter2.** Buen Abad Najar, Feng, Dai et al. bioRxiv (2025), [doi:10.1101/2025.04.06.646893](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.04.06.646893). Productive and unproductive splicing QTL in [ROSMAP DLPFC](xqtl-data/qtl/sQTL/ROSMAP_DLPFC_splicing_qtl), [PCC](xqtl-data/qtl/sQTL/ROSMAP_PCC_splicing_qtl) and [AC](xqtl-data/qtl/sQTL/ROSMAP_AC_splicing_qtl).
- **Reference-free isoform discovery links unannotated RNA processing to disease risk.** Buen Abad Najar, Xie, Feng et al. bioRxiv (2026), [doi:10.64898/2026.10.21.760529](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.21.760529). The Torino method behind the [ROSMAP tpQTL](xqtl-data/qtl/tpQTL/ROSMAP_tpQTL) results.
- **Single-cell splicing analysis with ISSAC links cell type-specific and cell state-dependent sQTLs to neurological disorders.** Zhang, Wang, Tan et al. Manuscript. The [ROSMAP single-nucleus sQTL](xqtl-data/qtl/sQTL/ROSMAP_snuc_splicing_qtl) results ([splicing data](xqtl-data/omics/splicing/ROSMAP_snuc_splicing)).
- **Genetic architecture of the human brain glycoproteome implicates TMEM106B and ACE glycosylation in Alzheimer's disease pathogenesis.** Feng, Wang, Cao et al. Manuscript. The [ROSMAP DLPFC glycoQTL](xqtl-data/qtl/glycoQTL/ROSMAP_DLPFC_glycoproteomics_qtl) results.

## Fine-mapping and colocalization

- **ColocBoost multi-trait colocalization.** Cao et al. medRxiv (2025), [doi:10.1101/2025.04.17.25326042](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.04.17.25326042). The colocalization models on every QTL dataset page. Protocol: [ColocBoost](https://github.com/StatFunGen/xqtl-protocol/blob/main/code/SoS/mnm_analysis/mnm_methods/colocboost.ipynb).
- **Integrative multi-omics QTL colocalization maps regulatory architecture in aging human brain.** Cao, Sun, Feng et al. Manuscript. ColocBoost colocalization across 17 ROSMAP xQTL datasets: cell-type and bulk eQTL ([single-nucleus](xqtl-data/qtl/eQTL/ROSMAP_snRNAseq_pseudo_bulk_qtl), [DLPFC](xqtl-data/qtl/eQTL/ROSMAP_DLPFC_expression_qtl), [PCC](xqtl-data/qtl/eQTL/ROSMAP_PCC_expression_qtl), [AC](xqtl-data/qtl/eQTL/ROSMAP_AC_expression_qtl), [monocyte](xqtl-data/qtl/eQTL/ROSMAP_monocyte_expression_qtl)), productive and unproductive sQTL in the three cortical regions, and [DLPFC pQTL](xqtl-data/qtl/pQTL/ROSMAP_DLPFC_proteomics_qtl).
- **mfSuSiE enables multi-cell-type fine-mapping and multi-omic integration of chromatin accessibility QTLs in aging brain.** Liu, Sun, De Jager et al. Manuscript. The [ROSMAP snATAC-seq caQTL](xqtl-data/qtl/caQTL/ROSMAP_snuc_caQTL_qtl) results. Protocol: [fSuSiE fine-mapping](https://github.com/StatFunGen/xqtl-protocol/blob/main/code/SoS/mnm_analysis/univariate_fine_mapping_fsusie_vignette.ipynb).
- **Small-sample calibration of SuSiE fine-mapping.** Denault et al. Manuscript. Integrates the residual variance out of each single-effect regression (SS-SER) so credible sets stay calibrated when there are fewer than about 50 samples, as in rare cell types.
- **Robust fine-mapping of molecular and complex traits with shareable LD references.** Sun, Cao, Zou et al. Manuscript. The [ADSP LD sketch](xqtl_resource_description) and [LD reference panels](xqtl-data/reference_data/ld_reference) (16,571 European-ancestry samples). Protocol: [RSS LD sketch](https://github.com/StatFunGen/xqtl-protocol/blob/main/code/SoS/reference_data/rss_ld_sketch.ipynb) and [summary-statistics fine-mapping](https://github.com/StatFunGen/xqtl-protocol/blob/main/code/SoS/mnm_analysis/mnm_methods/rss_analysis.ipynb).

## Integration with disease and other traits

- **Machine Learning-Based Prediction of Cell-type Resolved Brain eQTLs Enhances Discovery of Variants Explaining Alzheimer's Disease Heritability.** Lakhani, Cavalca, Liu et al. Manuscript. The [scEEMS scores](sceems_scores). Protocol: [EMS training](https://github.com/StatFunGen/xqtl-protocol/blob/main/code/SoS/xqtl_modifier_score/ems_training.ipynb).
- **Distributional genetic effects reveal context-dependent molecular regulation in human brain aging and Alzheimer's disease.** Liu, Jiang, Li et al. Manuscript. Quantile QTL and quantile TWAS (qTWAS) models, listed as not available yet on the dataset pages. Protocol: [quantile QTL and TWAS](https://github.com/StatFunGen/xqtl-protocol/blob/main/code/SoS/association_scan/quantile_models/qr_and_twas.ipynb).
- **A multi-scale cell-type resolved map of trans-regulatory architecture in the aging human brain.** Cao, Wang, Sun et al. Manuscript. The TransMap trans-xQTL evidence shown in the AD Loci Explorer.
- **Neuroimaging endophenotypes and xQTL (DNE-xQTL).** Li, Feng, Liu et al. Manuscript. Links dimensional neuroimaging endophenotypes of brain aging to the xQTL resources on this site.
