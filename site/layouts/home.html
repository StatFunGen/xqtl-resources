ADSP Functional Genomics Consortium (FunGen-AD)

FunGen-xQTL

FunGen-xQTL maps molecular QTL (xQTL) in eight modalities across brain and immune cells and integrates them with Alzheimer’s disease (AD) GWAS. This catalog lists each dataset and where to get it.
