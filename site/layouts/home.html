ADSP Functional Genomics Consortium · FunGen-AD

Find and access the FunGen-xQTL datasets

FunGen-xQTL maps genetic effects on gene expression, splicing, DNA methylation, proteins, chromatin and metabolites in brain and immune cells, and integrates them with AD GWAS. This catalog describes each dataset and where to get it. To look up associations by variant, gene or region, use the xQTL Atlas on NIAGADS.
