ADSP Functional Genomics Consortium (FunGen-AD)

FunGen-xQTL resource catalog

FunGen-xQTL fine-maps molecular QTL across eight molecular modalities, from chromatin and RNA to protein and metabolites, in brain and immune cells, and reuses the resulting credible sets and posterior effect estimates to interpret AD GWAS loci. This catalog describes each dataset and where to get it. To look up associations by variant, gene or region, use the NIAGADS xQTL Atlas.
